# Travel and Expense Policy

## Flights and hotels

Employees book economy flights and hotels within the published nightly rate cap. Bookings are made through the approved travel portal.

## Meals

A daily meal allowance of 75 dollars applies on overnight business travel. Alcohol is not reimbursed.

## Expense reports

Expense reports with receipts are submitted within 30 days of the trip. Late reports may be rejected.
