# Procurement and Purchase Order Policy

## Purchase order requirement

Purchases above 500 dollars require a purchase order approved before the goods or services are ordered. Invoices without a purchase order are returned to the supplier unless the finance manager approves an exception.

## Competitive quotes

Purchases above 25,000 dollars require at least three written quotes. Single source purchases require written justification approved by the director of procurement.

## Contracts and price tolerance

Contracts may set a supplier-specific price tolerance. Where a contract is in force on the invoice date its tolerance overrides the default of 2 percent.

## Goods receipt

Receiving staff post a goods receipt on the day goods arrive. Partial deliveries are posted as separate receipts against the purchase order line.
