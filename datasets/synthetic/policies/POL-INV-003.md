# Inventory and Receiving Procedure

## Receiving inspection

Goods are inspected on arrival for quantity and damage. Discrepancies are recorded on the goods receipt and the buyer is notified the same day.

## Returns

Goods rejected at inspection are returned to the supplier within ten business days and a credit note is requested.

## Over-deliveries

Quantities received above the purchase order quantity are not accepted without buyer approval.
