# Records Retention Schedule

## Financial records

Invoices, payments and supporting documents are kept for seven years from the end of the fiscal year in which they were created.

## Audit logs

System audit logs of approvals and payments are append-only and retained for ten years. Logs may not be edited or deleted.

## Disposal

Records past retention are destroyed securely and the destruction is logged.
