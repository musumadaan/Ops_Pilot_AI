# Acceptable Use and Data Security Policy

## Passwords and access

Passwords must be at least 14 characters and are protected by multi-factor authentication. Access is granted on a least-privilege basis and reviewed every quarter.

## Handling confidential data

Confidential supplier and financial data may be shared only with authorised staff and must not be pasted into public tools.

## AI tools

AI assistants may be used for drafting and analysis only on approved platforms. Outputs affecting payments must be reviewed by a person and must cite their sources.
