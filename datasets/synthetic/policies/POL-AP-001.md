# Accounts Payable Invoice Processing Policy

## Purpose and scope

This policy governs how supplier invoices are received, matched, approved and paid. It applies to all purchase order invoices in Canadian and US dollars.

## Three-way match

Every invoice line must be matched to a purchase order line and to goods received before payment. The invoiced quantity may not exceed the quantity received. Where goods have not been received the invoice is placed on hold until a goods receipt is posted.

## Price tolerance

The invoiced unit price may differ from the purchase order price by no more than 2 percent unless the supplier contract states a different tolerance. Differences above tolerance are sent back to the buyer for confirmation and are not paid until resolved.

## Invoice receipt and posting

Invoices are posted on business days. An invoice received on a Saturday or Sunday is flagged for review because weekend posting is outside normal supplier behaviour.

## Payment timing

Invoices are paid on or before the due date. Early payment discounts are taken only when approved by the finance manager.
