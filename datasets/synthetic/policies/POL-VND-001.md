# Supplier Master Data and Bank Account Changes

## Bank account change verification

A change of supplier bank account must be verified by calling the supplier on a phone number already held in the supplier master, never a number given in the change request. Payments to the new account are blocked until the callback is documented by two finance staff.

## Bank change on invoice

An invoice that quotes a different bank account from the previous invoice of the same supplier is placed on hold and treated as potential payment fraud until verified.

## Supplier onboarding

New suppliers provide a tax registration, a void cheque or bank letter and two references. Onboarding is approved by procurement and finance.

## Supplier hold

A supplier placed on hold may not receive new purchase orders and open invoices are paid only with finance manager approval.
