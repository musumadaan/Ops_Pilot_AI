# Duplicate and Split Invoice Controls

## Duplicate invoice numbers

Two invoices from the same supplier with the same invoice number, ignoring spaces, dashes and letter case, are treated as duplicates. The later invoice is held and compared with the original before any payment is made.

## Possible duplicates by amount

Invoices from the same supplier with an identical total amount received within 14 days of each other are reviewed as possible duplicates even when the invoice numbers differ.

## Split invoices

Splitting a purchase into several invoices just below the approval threshold to avoid higher approval is prohibited. Two or more invoices from one supplier between 80 percent and 100 percent of the threshold within 7 days are escalated to the finance manager and reviewed against their combined value.

## Round amounts

Invoice totals that are exact multiples of 1,000 dollars are an information flag. They are not blocked, but the reviewer confirms the amount against the purchase order.
