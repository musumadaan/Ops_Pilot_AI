# Fraud Investigation Procedure

## Escalation

Suspected invoice fraud is reported to the finance manager within one business day. Payments are frozen and evidence is preserved before the supplier is contacted.

## Evidence handling

Every investigation records the source documents, query results and reviewers. Evidence must be reproducible: queries are saved with parameters and the time they were run.

## Whistleblower protection

Employees who report suspected fraud in good faith are protected from retaliation. Reports may be made anonymously through the ethics line.
