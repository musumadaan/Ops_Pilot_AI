# Approval Authority Matrix

## Approval threshold

Invoices of 10,000 dollars or more require approval above the buyer level. The invoice is approved by the lowest person in the buyer's management chain whose approval limit covers the invoice total.

## Approval limits

Buyers may approve up to 10,000 dollars, managers up to 50,000 dollars, directors up to 250,000 dollars and the executive up to 1,000,000 dollars. Amounts above 1,000,000 dollars require board approval.

## Segregation of duties

The person who created a purchase order may not approve the invoice against it when the invoice is above the buyer limit. Approvals are recorded with the approver, time and reason.

## Delegation

An approver absent for more than five business days must delegate in writing to a person of equal or higher limit. Delegations are recorded in the approval log.
